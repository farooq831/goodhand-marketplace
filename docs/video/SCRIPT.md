# Goodhand walkthrough — narration script

Presenter: **Muhammad Farooq, Founder**. Length about 5 min 17 s, 18 scenes.

## Re-recording in your own voice

1. Record each scene below as its own WAV file. Read it naturally; any length is fine.
2. Save each file over the one in `audio/` with the same name, for example `audio/s01-title.wav`.
3. Run `node assemble.mjs`.

The video is re-fitted to your recordings automatically, and the subtitles are re-timed. To change wording, edit `scenes.json`.

## 1. s01-title
*On screen: Title slide — Muhammad Farooq, Founder*

Assalamu Alaikum, and welcome. I'm Muhammad Farooq, the founder of Goodhand. In the next few minutes, I'll show you how Goodhand makes finding, booking and paying for local services simple, safe and fair, for customers and for service providers alike.

## 2. s02-problem
*On screen: The problem today (4 cards)*

Today, most of us find a tutor, an electrician or a photographer through WhatsApp groups and word of mouth. There are no real reviews, no easy way to compare prices, payments happen in cash with no protection, and providers juggle bookings in paper diaries. Goodhand fixes all of that.

## 3. s03-home
*On screen: Homepage, categories, trust section (guest)*

This is Goodhand. Every provider you see here has been checked by our team, and every payment is protected. You can search for anything you need, or start from a category, like tutoring, home repair, photography, events or cleaning.

## 4. s04-search
*On screen: Search: trust-score ranking, filters, save*

Search results are ranked by our trust score, not just star ratings. It rewards providers who reply quickly, finish what they accept, and keep their customers happy. You can filter by price, rating, distance, and the exact day you need help, and save your favourites with a single tap.

## 5. s05-listing
*On screen: Customer logs in → service page → date → free slots*

Every service page shows real photos, the price, the duration, and reviews from customers who actually completed a booking. Pick a date, and you only see the time slots that are genuinely free. No more double bookings.

## 6. s06-checkout
*On screen: Checkout: address, notes → escrow payment*

At checkout, you tell the provider where to come and what the job involves. Then comes the most important part. Your payment is held safely in escrow. The provider is paid only after the work is done, and if they decline, or you cancel, you get a full refund.

## 7. s07-booking
*On screen: Booking page: status, address, private chat*

After booking, everything lives in one place. The status of your job, the address, and a private chat with your provider. You get notifications, plus reminders a day before and two hours before, so nobody forgets.

## 8. s08-customer
*On screen: Customer dashboard: bookings, saved*

Your dashboard keeps your bookings, messages and saved providers together. When the job is done, you accept the work, and leave an honest review that helps the whole community.

## 9. s09-vendor-dash
*On screen: Provider logs in → dashboard stats & trust score*

Now let me show you the other side. If you are a service provider, Goodhand is your business toolkit. Your dashboard shows how many people viewed your services, your acceptance and completion rates, your earnings this month, and your trust score.

## 10. s10-vendor-accept
*On screen: Accept a paid request → calendar & time off*

New requests arrive already paid, so you never waste time on fake bookings. Accept with one click, and the slot is blocked on your calendar automatically. Going on holiday? Just block those days, and customers can't book them.

## 11. s11-vendor-earnings
*On screen: Earnings: held / awaiting payout / paid out*

Your earnings page shows exactly where every rupee is. Held in escrow, awaiting payout, or paid out, with a transaction reference. Add your bank account, JazzCash or Easypaisa once, and we send your money there.

## 12. s12-vendor-verify
*On screen: Vendor profile & CNIC verification*

To join, providers add their business details and upload their CNIC. This verification is what lets customers trust you from day one, and it keeps fake accounts out of the marketplace.

## 13. s13-admin-verify
*On screen: Admin logs in → vendor review drawer*

Behind the scenes, our admin team reviews every application. They can see all the details and documents, and either approve the provider, or ask for specific corrections, which are sent straight to the provider by email.

## 14. s14-admin-dispute
*On screen: Dispute drawer: reason, chat, release/refund*

If something ever goes wrong, either side can raise a dispute. The admin sees the full chat history and timeline, and decides fairly whether to release the payment to the provider, or refund the customer.

## 15. s15-admin-ops
*On screen: Payouts, listing moderation, security & audit*

Admins also manage payouts, moderate listings, and watch a security dashboard that flags suspicious logins. Every important action is recorded in an audit trail, so the platform stays accountable.

## 16. s16-devices
*On screen: Three phones: light, dark, any screen*

And Goodhand works beautifully on any device, in light or dark mode, because most of our customers browse on their phones.

## 17. s17-benefits
*On screen: Why move to Goodhand (benefits slide)*

So, why move to Goodhand? For customers: verified providers, honest reviews, and payments that are protected until the job is done. For providers: real, paid bookings, a calendar that prevents double booking, and a reputation that grows with every happy customer.

## 18. s18-end
*On screen: Call to action — create a free account*

Join Goodhand today. Create a free account, as a customer or as a provider, and experience the safer way to get things done. Thank you for watching. I'm Muhammad Farooq, and I'll see you on Goodhand.
